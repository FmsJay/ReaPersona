# Themes the styles use, with credit

**ReaPersona's own themes** (one per DAW) are built on your computer by Setup, from REAPER's Default 7 (© Cockos), which every REAPER has. The recipes for them are in `styles.json` under `own_themes`.

Every other theme here belongs to its author and isn't redistributed. Get it from the link, then drop the file (or the pack's zip) onto ReaPersona's window. A style uses the first of its themes you have, and the Theme list lets you pick another.
